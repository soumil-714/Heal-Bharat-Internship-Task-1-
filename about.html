Who Are We?

We're not just another hackathon team. We're a group of innovators, coders, designers, and dreamers who believe in building solutions that matter. Every hackathon is a new challenge, a new adventure, and a new chance to push boundaries.

🚀 Our Mission

To create impactful tech solutions that solve real-world problems — whether it's optimizing energy, enabling rural healthcare, or building secure voting platforms. We aim to learn fast, fail forward, and ship smart.

🛠️ What We Do

We specialize in full-stack development, IoT integrations, AI/ML, and creative problem-solving under pressure. Each member brings a unique superpower, and together we build projects that turn heads and win hearts.

💡 Our Ethos

We believe in collaboration over competition, learning by doing, and that no idea is too big or too wild. Our hackathons are a place where ideas grow into code — fast.

🤝 Join the Squad

Whether you're a developer, designer, or just someone crazy about tech, we'd love to have you on board. Let’s build something epic together. Join us, and let’s hack the future.
